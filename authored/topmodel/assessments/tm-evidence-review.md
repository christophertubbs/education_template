# Initial evidence review

Eligible canonical records: `tm-evidence-q1`, `tm-evidence-q2`,
`tm-evidence-q3`, `tm-evidence-q4`, `tm-evidence-q5`, `tm-evidence-q6`, all in
`questions/tm-evidence-bank.md` at their matching Record anchors.

Every item maps to `tm-evidence`, `tm-evidence-contract`, `tm-verify-claims`,
and review lesson `tm-evidence-lesson`. Restrict eligibility to completion of
that lesson. Select three distinct items; each carries one point with no partial
credit. There are twenty unordered three-item subsets. Every subset preserves
the one concept/contract/outcome covered by this introductory bank.

Scenarios vary the evidence problem: an unexecuted test, a fixture limitation,
old documentation, an invalid scientific inference, a missing revision, or a
status report that confuses failure to execute with failure of the science.
These are distinct reasoning cases rather than shuffled versions of one prompt.
They support early review, not a comprehensive examination or complete mastery
of all testing and scientific-evaluation topics.
